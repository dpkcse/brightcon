# Client handover policy

A buyer may deliver one modified Buildora CMS installation as part of one completed website to one end client. The buyer must give the client `LICENSE` and required third-party notices and ensure the client understands that the license remains permanently assigned to that website and normalized production domain.

Handover does not create a second license. The buyer may retain a non-production backup only for support, recovery, or deployment preparation for that same website and may not reuse it for another project. Neither party may independently sublicense, resell, publish, or redistribute the CMS. Transfer of client content or buyer-created custom code does not transfer ownership of Buildora CMS core code. `LICENSE` controls.
