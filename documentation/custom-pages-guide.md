# Custom pages
Administrators manage pages under **Content → Custom Pages**. Draft and archived pages return 404 publicly; preview remains admin-only. Slugs are normalized, unique, and cannot use centralized system-reserved values. Content accepts only `p`, `h2`–`h4`, `ul`, `ol`, `li`, `strong`, `em`, `blockquote`, `a`, and `br`. Attributes are removed; safe links are retained and external HTTP(S) links receive `target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"`. Images are optional raster files. A referenced page cannot be deleted until menu links are removed; archive it to withdraw it safely.
